# Buyer Personas Reference

Guidelines and archetypes for constructing realistic, high-signal prospective buyers.

## Persona Archetypes

### 1. The DTC / Shopify Brand Owner
- **Role:** Founder, Solopreneur, or Head of Growth at an e-commerce brand ($200k–$3M GMV).
- **Day-to-day:** Juggling Facebook/Google ad spend, supply chain delays, Shopify theme edits, customer support.
- **Budget Authority:** Spends freely on high-ROI apps up to $50–$100/mo without asking anyone; freezes if pricing requires a demo or contract.
- **Trigger:** Ad ROAS dropped this week, products got disapproved on Google Merchant Center, or holiday inventory crunch is looming.
- **Scar Tissue:** "AI apps" that promised auto-fixes but ruined product listings or required 10 hours of manual cleanup.

### 2. The Mid-Market B2B SaaS Buyer
- **Role:** Director of Product, VP of Engineering, or Operations Manager (50–300 employees).
- **Day-to-day:** Meetings, cross-functional alignment, vendor risk assessments, hitting quarterly OKRs.
- **Budget Authority:** Needs manager approval or finance sign-off for anything recurring over $500/year.
- **Trigger:** Current tool is sunsetting, security audit identified a gap, or CEO demanded a progress report.
- **Scar Tissue:** Shelfware tools with 6-month onboarding and bad export capabilities.

### 3. The Freelancer / Boutique Agency Owner
- **Role:** Solo consultant or 3-5 person agency serving SMB clients.
- **Day-to-day:** Client deliverables, pitching new retainers, chasing invoices.
- **Budget Authority:** Very price-sensitive ($10-$30/mo); cost must be billable to clients.
- **Trigger:** Needs to audit a prospect's store in a sales pitch or automate a tedious audit checklist.
- **Scar Tissue:** Tools that claim "client report generation" but output ugly or non-white-labeled PDFs.

## How to Select the Right Persona
1. **Match the Pricing:** Look at the lowest paid tier. If it's $19/mo, pick the self-serve solopreneur or agency. If it's $499/mo, pick the departmental manager.
2. **Match the Integration Requirement:** Does it need store access, read-only feeds, or private API tokens? The more access needed, the more skeptical and protective the persona must be.
